Fibre Hologram Display

  1. Turn screen brightness to maximum and dim the room.
  2. Tap Start, then lay the phone or tablet flat, screen up.
  3. Stand the clear pyramid upside down, small end on the dashed square in the middle.
  4. Look at the pyramid from the side, at screen height. The ball appears inside it.
  5. Tap the screen any time to show or hide the controls.

How it works
  1. Fibres are chains. Each one is 22 points. Every point chases its place on the current form with a spring.
  2. Tips are loose. The spring is strong at the root and weak at the tip, so tips lag, whip and trail when the form spins or changes.
  3. Forms are formulas. Shell, orb, jellyfish and the rest are just functions of “which fibre” and “how far along it”. Morphing blends two of them.
  4. Burst cuts the springs for a moment and kicks every point outward. The springs then pull the cloud back into shape.
  5. Glow is a blur. The fibres are drawn again into a small image, blurred, and added on top. A mirrored copy feeds the desk reflection.
